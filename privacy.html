Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you visit tikitala.com or play TIKITALA Online, and how it is used.

What we don't collect

TIKITALA does not have user accounts, does not require sign-up, and does not collect names, email addresses, or any personal information to let you browse the site or play the game.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand, in aggregate, how many people visit and play, and which pages are popular. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish visitors between visits.

TIKITALA Online also reports a few anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics, so we can see how the game is played and where it could be better:

  • when a game starts and ends, with the mode (against the computer or pass & play), the computer level, the number of moves, and which side won
  • when the how-to-play lessons are started and completed

These events don't include your name, anything you type, or any other personal information.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data. You can opt out of Google Analytics on every site with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser; the game works the same either way.

Local storage

TIKITALA Online uses your browser's local storage to remember a few things between visits:

  • games in progress, against the computer or pass & play, so you can pick up where you left off (including what the computer remembers about the board)
  • the computer level you last played
  • whether you've finished or skipped the how-to-play lessons
  • your language, if you picked one different from your browser's

This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. A finished game is removed automatically, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

The Share button at the end of a game hands a short result message and a link to the game to your device's share menu, or copies it to your clipboard. Where it goes from there is up to you; it isn't sent to us.

Other third-party services

The site loads a small number of third-party resources to function and to display content:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), for the site's and the game's typefaces
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), for the homepage's menu icons
  • A Twitter/X embedded post on the homepage, which may set its own cookies once loaded
  • Outbound links to Etsy, Kickstarter, GitHub Sponsors, and Ko-fi, each governed by their own privacy policies once you click through

Advertising

TIKITALA does not currently show advertising. If that changes in the future (for example, via Google AdSense), this policy will be updated first to describe the advertising cookies in use, and visitors in the EEA, UK, and other regions that require it will be shown a consent choice before any ad or ad-measurement cookie is set.

Children's privacy

TIKITALA is a general-audience game and site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the TIKITALA GitHub repository.
